## Motivation

Nice to meet you, I'm mitz17.  
I finally started a blog.

I plan to write casual notes about:

- small programs that make everyday life a bit better
- random life hacks I come up with
- build logs of things I made

## Why I Decided to Start

There's something a little cool about owning your own domain.

That vibe alone made me register one quickly.
After that, though, I did not have a huge grand vision.

But domains have a maintenance cost.

I'm terrible at dealing with fixed costs that keep draining money every month.

So I thought:

If ads on this blog can at least cover the domain fee, that's enough.

There is a chance I lose motivation halfway through.
Still, I think it will be fun if that process itself remains as a log.

## Goal

Cover the domain maintenance fee with ad revenue from this blog.

That's my first step.
